SleepNow AI — Delete Your Account & Data

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

SleepNow AI works completely with no account — your sleep record lives on your device. An account is optional and exists only to back that record up. You can delete either or both at any time, right in the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your data on this device (no account needed)

  1. Open SleepNow AI and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This erases your entire record from this device immediately — nightly check-ins, sleep score history, settings, and saved sound mixes. Removing the app also removes its on-device data.

Delete your account and its cloud backup (if you signed in)

  1. Open SleepNow AI and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes the backup copy stored on our server (provided by Supabase) and removes your account itself. If you also want the record gone from this phone, use Delete all my data above as well.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email info@annizon.com with the subject “Delete my SleepNow AI account” and tell us the email address you used to sign in. We will delete your account and its stored backup and confirm by reply. Data that only ever existed on a device you no longer have access to is removed with the app and cannot be recovered or deleted by us — we never had a copy.

What is not deleted by these steps

Deleting your data or account does not cancel a SleepNow Plus subscription billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play — cancel it in your store's subscription settings to stop future charges. Purchase records are held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies; we receive subscription status only, never your card details. AI pattern summaries are generated from on-device averages and totals only — no identifiable record is kept from them.
