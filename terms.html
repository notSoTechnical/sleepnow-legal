Effective: November 13, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

A PERSONAL RECORD, NOT MEDICAL ADVICE SleepNow AI logs your nights, scores them with a formula it shows you, and notices patterns in your numbers. It is not a medical device, it does not detect or diagnose any condition (including sleep apnea), and it does not give medical advice or recommend any treatment. The AI pattern summary describes your recorded numbers only — it does not advise, recommend, or interpret what a pattern means. Questions about your sleep belong to you and your clinician.

THE BODY CLOCK AND WIND-DOWN CONTENT The body clock presents the traditional Chinese medicine organ clock (the two-hour windows traditionally associated with each organ) and the wind-down acupressure cards describe points and their traditional uses. This content is educational and reflects traditional practice only — it is not a medical claim, a diagnosis, or a treatment, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified practitioner.

YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES Enter your nights as they happened, and treat the score and the summaries as a record of your own numbers — not as a measurement of your health. The bedtime reminder is a memory aid only.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS Your record is stored on your device and can be exported in full at any time, free. The optional free account also keeps a synced backup, so a new phone can pick the record up.

SLEEPNOW PLUS (SUBSCRIPTION) Plus is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID: US$4.99 per month or US$29.99 per year (prices in other currencies are shown on the purchase screen). It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and you can manage or cancel it any time in your Apple ID / App Store account settings. Plus adds the AI pattern summaries (your first summary is free) and the 30- and 90-night trend views. Logging, history, your sleep score, the daily tip, all sleep sounds with the mixer and fade-out timer, the body clock, the wind-down cards, Apple Health sleep import, and all exports are free and never require Plus.
