SleepNow AI — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Annizon · Contact: info@annizon.com

SleepNow AI is built to be private by design.

WHERE YOUR RECORD LIVES Everything you log — your nightly check-ins (sleep times, quality ratings, wake-ups, tags like caffeine or screens, and notes), your sleep score history, your settings, and your saved sound mixes — is stored on this device, in the app's own storage. The app works completely with no account, and we cannot see your record.

OPTIONAL ACCOUNT (BACKUP & SYNC) If you choose to sign in in Settings — with an email sign-in link, no password — the same record is also stored in our database (provided by Supabase) in a row tied to your account, protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. It is used for exactly two things: backing your record up, and restoring it on a new phone when you sign in there. That is the only copy that ever leaves your device, and only while you are signed in. Signing out stops syncing; "Delete account" in Settings removes your account and the stored backup. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no sale or sharing of your data — there is nothing to sell, and we do not do it.

PURCHASES (SLEEPNOW PLUS) If you subscribe to SleepNow Plus, the purchase is processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information only — never your sleep entries, notes, or any other part of your record.

AI PATTERN SUMMARY (PLUS) If you ask for an AI pattern summary, the app first computes averages and totals from your log on this phone, and sends ONLY those numbers (average score and duration, average quality, wake-up counts, how your tagged nights compare) to our server, which uses Google's Gemini AI to turn them into a plain-language summary. Your daily entries, your notes, your name, and your email are never sent — the summary request carries no identity at all.

APPLE HEALTH (OPTIONAL) If you turn it on in Settings, the app reads your sleep from Apple Health so nights recorded by a watch, a ring, or another app can appear in your record with their durations and stages. The import is read-only — the app never writes to Apple Health — and the imported nights become part of your record (and of your account backup, if you use one). Nights you check in yourself are never changed by an import, and your own ratings always take priority.

NOTIFICATIONS The app only sends the one notification you create yourself — the bedtime reminder you set in Settings. There are no promotional or "we miss you" notifications.

DELETING YOUR RECORD Settings → "Delete all my data" erases the entire record from this device immediately and permanently. Removing the app also removes its data. The account backup is deleted separately, under Settings → Account → "Delete account", which also removes your account itself.
